ABOUT AKWAYA

Remote, touristic — and rich in forest & culture

Akwaya is a frontier council at the edge of the Takamanda forest, shaped by rivers, highlands and community. Far from the noise of the city, it is a place where nature, heritage and everyday life still move together.

Two faces, one Akwaya: dense forest in South (Takamanda, Mone) and savannah in North (Obudu hills).

Because of the ongoing crisis in Akwaya Subdivision, the Council Office currently operates from Mamfe. Services, civil documents and enquiries are still available there — close, official and welcoming to every family.
